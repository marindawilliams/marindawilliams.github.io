Questions people ask

Where exactly is The Fields?

In District 11 of Mohammed Bin Rashid City, DLD zone Wadi Al Safa 3, near Meydan. Al Ain Road (E66) and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311) are close by, so Downtown, Business Bay and the airport are an easy drive outside peak hours.

How much does it cost to rent in The Fields?

New leases registered in 2026 averaged AED 168,000 a year and renewals AED 180,000, across all unit sizes. The rent for a specific home depends on bedrooms, size and position: corner, single-row and park-facing units lease faster.

Is it a good fit for families?

It is designed around families: gated access with 24/7 security, parks, pools, play areas, jogging and cycling tracks, and homes with private gardens and a maid's room. Schools and retail are a short drive away.

What should an investor look at?

Compare recorded sale prices with registered rents for the same sub-community and layout, then subtract service charges from the DLD Mollak index and a realistic empty period. Position matters: corner and park-facing units lease faster.

I want to rent out my townhouse. Where do I start?

Start with the right rent: compare registered Ejari rents for your exact layout, not the highest listing. Fix small snags, photograph in daylight, and decide how you'll screen tenants. Marinda offers a free rental valuation.

How do landlords avoid bad tenants?

Screen before signing. Marinda runs an AECB credit check on every tenant she places, alongside employment and reference checks.
